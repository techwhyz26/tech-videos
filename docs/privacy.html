Privacy Policy

TechWhyz Uploader. Last updated 27 November 2026.

What the app is

TechWhyz Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the TechWhyz YouTube channel. It uploads the channel's own short videos to that channel and sets their title, description, tags and publish time. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else signs in to it.

What data it uses

  • Through the YouTube API Services, it uses the TechWhyz channel's permission to upload videos and to read the channel's own name and ID, so it can confirm it is uploading to the right channel.
  • The sign-in token is stored as an encrypted secret in the channel owner's private settings and is used only to upload the owner's own videos.

The app does not collect, store, sell or share information about viewers or any other users. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising.

YouTube and Google

TechWhyz Uploader uses the YouTube API Services. By using it you are also bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Access can be removed at any time at Google security settings.
